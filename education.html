Academic Output

PUBLICATIONS.

Dissertation (Portuguese Only)
"Computers Make Art" — Didactics for Web3
Master's dissertation investigating pedagogical frameworks for teaching blockchain, NFTs, and Web3 to diverse audiences. Developed at UFPE, it bridges design education and emerging digital technologies.
↗
Paper
At the frontier of the metaverse: NFTs, artistic expression, and digital immersions
Explores NFTs beyond digital art tokenization, examining the tension between artistic autonomy and commercialization in Web3. Published in the Metaverse journal (2024), with case studies from immersive exhibitions.
↗
Paper
Metaverse and cultural preservation: an alternative through NFTs of social sustainability in Brazil
Presented at SVR'23, this paper investigates how NFTs and metaverse platforms can be used to preserve and promote Indigenous cultural heritage through authorship assurance.
↗
Book Chapter (Portuguese Only)
"Computers Make Art" — Blockchain & NFTs
Chapter from the collection of short courses from the WebMedia 2024 event. Translates blockchain and NFT concepts into an accessible educational language, result of my master's degree on teaching Web3 through artistic and design practice.

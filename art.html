Metaverse & in-person shows

I'm Numa, a Brazilian crypto artist and visual artist. I've drawn for as long as I can remember, and that instinct grew into a whole visual language: an oneiric, extremely colorful universe where fantasy, culture, and mainly Brazilian fauna and flora merge into hybrid creatures.

My practice moves fluidly across mediums — drawing and graffiti, handmade sculpture, 3D, and virtual reality. The same beings that live on a wall find new form on-chain and inside immersive worlds.

My work has been exhibited both in the metaverse and in person, across several states in Brazil and in countries such as France, Thailand, and Germany.

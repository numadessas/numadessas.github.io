OVERVIEW

One world,
many products.

3.land is a Web3 product studio building and experimenting on Solana. I shaped the studio's visual identity and site — a cyberspace of neon grids, an alien mascot, and an acid-green / violet / magenta palette that ties every product into a single language.

The real challenge was narrative: telling a four-year arc —
metaverse → marketplace → tooling → game
— as one coherent story instead of four disconnected launches.

01 / METAVERSE

Where it started

A 3D virtual gallery to showcase NFTs, presented at the Breakpoint Amsterdam VR Exhibition and Art Basel. This is where the identity was born: the alien mascot, the retrowave grid, the neon.

Presented Breakpoint AmsterdamAlso Art Basel · VR ExhibitionsBuild 3D · WebGL
02 / MARKETPLACE

A launchpad for creators

A Solana-based NFT launchpad — creators mint, list, and sell in any SPL token. Art direction for the key visuals: liquid gradients floating inside an infinite grid room.

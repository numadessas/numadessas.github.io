OVERVIEW

One world,
many products.

3.land was a Web3 product studio that rapidly prototyped, launched, and iterated on Solana. Joining as the first employee, I shaped the studio's visual identity and site — a cyberspace of neon grids, an alien mascot, and an acid-green / violet / magenta palette that tied every product into a single language.

The real challenge was narrative: telling a four-year arc —
metaverse → marketplace → tooling → game
— as one coherent story instead of four disconnected launches, across five languages from day one.

01 / METAVERSE

Where it started.

3.land began as a virtual metaverse where artists built immersive 3D galleries for their NFT collections. It brought an immersive VR exhibition to Breakpoint Amsterdam 2023 and built Solana's virtual art gallery for Art Basel 2024. This is where the identity was born: the alien mascot, the retrowave grid, the neon.

VR Breakpoint Amsterdam '23Gallery Art Basel '24Build 3D · WebGL
02 / MARKETPLACE

A launchpad for creators.

After the metaverse, the team pivoted to one of the first multilingual NFT marketplaces on Solana, later specialized in compressed NFTs (cNFTs). It supported on-chain traits, any SPL token as payment, and was one of the first to integrate Blinks — surpassing 100,000 NFTs minted through Blink-powered flows. I art-directed the key visuals: liquid gradients floating inside an infinite grid room.

04 / MY ROLE

What I did.

As the studio's first employee, I wore many hats. Over four years my role spanned direction, production, and the systems that kept a small team shipping fast.

01

Art Direction

Photoshop · Illustrator

Set the visual direction for launches and campaigns — key visuals, logos, banners, and card art — keeping every product on one language.

02

3D

Blender · glTF

Working alongside two other artists, I animated and rendered the studio's 3D graphics — from the alien mascot to 3D logos and scene art. They led the modeling; I focused on final production, jumping into modeling myself when needed.

03

Motion & Audio

Premiere · After Effects

Edited promo videos, event recaps, and interviews — cutting footage, animating, and building sound design from a custom SFX library.

04

Social Strategy

Content · Strategy

Collaborated on the social media strategy — content planning, campaign rollouts, and milestone moments.

05

Team & Process

Ops · Systems

Part of a small, international team — two co-founders (project management and engineering), another developer, two 3D artists, and a product designer. Beyond the visuals, I set up the company's first file-organization system and introduced Trello to structure communication — a workflow the developers later adopted as well.

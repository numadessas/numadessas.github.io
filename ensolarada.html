OVERVIEW

Art & NFTs under the sun.

A project about decentralizing NFT culture — valuing and paying artists from Brazil, far from the usual art-and-crypto hubs.

It happened through photographer JT Liss's initiative to promote art events on Solana around the world, with initial support from the Solana Foundation — plus digital and local communities, NFT art projects and collectors.

01

Visual Identity

Illustrator · Photoshop · After Effects

Guest artist Matheus Ciberdelia created the main art, “La Vision”, to represent the event. I unfolded it into the whole communication: I designed the logo — a hand-drawn badge with a lime sun rising over a red hill — set the palette, and cut the art into elements that I animated across posts, stories, screens, plaques, tickets and the metaverse.

02

Production

Planning · Sponsorship · Logistics

I wrote the project and sponsorship deck (media, screen and exclusive-screen quotas), partnered with Casa Criatura in Olinda's historic center, invited the artists, set up the NFT drops — 1/1 pieces on Mallow and 100-edition works on 3.land — and ran a full opening day. Every collector got a commemorative NFT.

03

Social Media

Instagram · X · Stories

I planned and designed the whole rollout for Instagram and X: educational carousels (“What's an NFT?”, “What's a blockchain?”) for an audience new to crypto, then the artist line-up, venue, sponsors, tickets and program.

Recap video by Subverso Lab

04

Curation

Selection · Concept · Display

Every artist was born or lives in Brazil's Northeast, and each created two exclusive works for the show — a 1/1 and a 100-edition piece — displayed on screens across the house. I mixed NFT veterans with newcomers, bringing the Estelita label and Casa Criatura into the digital scene for the first time.
